# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the project.
Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributions that don't
follow this, and may block anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community.

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to [lfr@tik-ai.dk](mailto:lfr@tik-ai.dk).

This is a short adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
